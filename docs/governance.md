# Equal ownership and decisions

Darshan leads Cyber/backend; Rishitha leads AI/ML/React. Each has final say within
their track; cross-track choices require both. If deadlocked, write options and a
measurable criterion, run a spike of at most one relative working week and choose
the option meeting it. If unresolved, ask the supervisor/agreed neutral mentor.

Before public launch, both must review university student-IP rules and complete a
written agreement covering ownership/revenue/equity, Apache-2.0 licence choice,
DCO versus CLA, report/paper authorship, account/domain/package custody, departure
scenario and commercial offers. This checklist is a draft, not a signed agreement.
Both should hold appropriate organisation ownership with 2FA and safe recovery;
no membership, credentials or security settings are changed by this PR.

Reviews, contracts, docs, releases and community work are shared. Existing teams
and CODEOWNERS are preserved. Both review monthly gates and scope cuts. Plans are
relative phases at free-time capacity, with no calendar start/due dates.
