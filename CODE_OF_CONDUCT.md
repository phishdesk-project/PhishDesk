# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, give actionable feedback and
credit contributions. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private data
and unsafe testing against others' systems are unacceptable.

Use synthetic examples and authorised lab accounts. Report conduct concerns privately
through an established channel to either existing maintainer, Darshan or Rishitha.
If a maintainer is involved, contact the other owner or the agreed neutral mentor.
Do not publish private reports in issues. Maintainers will consider context, hear
affected people and use proportionate responses. A verified public reporting channel
must be published before public community launch.
